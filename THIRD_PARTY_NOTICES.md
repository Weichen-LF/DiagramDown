# Third-party notices

DiagramDown does not bundle Mermaid or D2. Diagram rendering uses optional
user-installed `mmdc` and `d2` command-line tools.

DiagramDown bundles Swift Markdown, Swift CMark, SwiftTreeSitter, Tree-sitter,
and grammar packages for Swift, Lua, YAML, JavaScript, TypeScript, JSON, Bash,
Python, Go, Dockerfile, and SQL/BigQuery syntax highlighting. Their license
texts are included in `Mark/Resources/Licenses` and in every packaged app.
