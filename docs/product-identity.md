# DiagramDown product identity

## Working name

**DiagramDown** combines the two defining parts of the product: diagrams and Markdown. It is descriptive enough for an early release while still feeling like a product name rather than a feature label.

This is a working name. Before a public release, repeat App Store, domain, and trademark checks in the intended distribution regions.

## App icon

The icon connects a document page to three diagram nodes. It represents prose flowing directly into Mermaid and D2 diagrams without leaving the editor.

The icon should remain recognizable at small sizes:

- No letters or fine text
- One strong document silhouette
- Three visible nodes and simple connectors
- Indigo and violet background with an off-white foreground
- Important artwork contained inside the macOS icon safe area

The source-sized app icon is `Mark/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/icon_512x512@2x.png`. Smaller catalog entries are derived from that artwork.

## Color

- Primary violet: `#6E4FE0`
- Deep indigo: `#17134F`
- Document white: `#F7F5FC`

The primary violet is also configured as the app accent color.

## Product voice

DiagramDown should feel native, quiet, and technical. Interface copy should be concise and direct, with diagram errors explained where they occur rather than hidden behind generic alerts.
